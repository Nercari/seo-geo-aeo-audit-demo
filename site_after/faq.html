How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?

Turn off every tap and check your water meter. If the dial still moves after 15 minutes, you likely have a leak. Call us and we will locate it.

How long does a water heater replacement take?

Most tank water heaters are replaced in 3 to 4 hours. A tankless install usually takes one working day.
