Plumbing Services in Marlowe Falls

We repair and install the plumbing in homes and small businesses. Each price below is a typical range and is confirmed in writing before we start.

Drain cleaning

We clear most blocked drains in under an hour. Typical cost: $120 to $250.

Leak repair

We find the leak, shut it off and repair or replace the damaged pipe, usually in one visit. Typical cost: $150 to $450.

Water heater replacement

We remove the old unit and install a new tank or tankless heater, usually in 3 to 4 hours for a tank. Typical cost: $1,100 to $2,400 installed.

Fixture installation

We fit toilets, sinks, faucets and showers and test for leaks before we leave. Typical cost: $140 to $320 per fixture, parts extra.
